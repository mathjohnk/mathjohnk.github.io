Biography

Academic Background

I received a Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences from Carnegie Mellon University in 2003. Afterwards I held postdoctoral positions at the University of Vienna and the University of California, Berkeley. I came to UNT in 2009 as an assistant professor. I was promoted to associate professor with tenure in 2015 and to full professor in 2021.
