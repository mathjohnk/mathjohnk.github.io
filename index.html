Professional Biography

Academic Background

I received a Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences from Carnegie Mellon University in 2003. Afterwards, I held postdoctoral positions at the University of Vienna and the University of California, Berkeley. In 2009, I joined the Department of Mathematics at the University of North Texas as an assistant professor. I was promoted to associate professor with tenure in 2015 and to full professor in 2021. I served as Director of Graduate Studies from Fall 2022 through Spring 2026.
